kterm for Kindle, GPL-3.0.
Based on kterm 2.6 by Bartek Fabiszewski: https://github.com/bfabiszewski/kterm
Changes: GTK+ 3 and VTE 0.84 on the Kindle X server, compact keyboard layout,
shortcut bar, swipe-up panel (SSH connections, shortcuts, font size, reading
mode, rotation), system keyboard (pinyin) bridge, KPM packaging.
The complete modified source accompanies this package as source.tar.gz.
