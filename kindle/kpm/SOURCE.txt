kterm for Kindle, GPL-3.0.
Based on kterm 2.6 by Bartek Fabiszewski: https://github.com/bfabiszewski/kterm
Changes: GTK+ 3 and VTE 0.84 on the Kindle X server, compact keyboard layout
(split in two halves in landscape), shortcut bar with panel and keyboard
buttons and prefix shortcuts, panel (SSH connections and disconnecting;
settings: font size, screen orientation, refresh, full refresh, shortcuts),
preview mode (taps on the top or bottom third turn a page), touch gestures
(scrolling, long press to copy, paste button while the program waits for
input, two finger right click), fewer screen refreshes to save the battery,
full refreshes clearing ghosting, system keyboard (pinyin) bridge, KPM
packaging. Icons: Material icons by Google (Apache 2.0).
The complete modified source accompanies this package as source.tar.gz.

libvte-2.91.so.0 is VTE 0.84.0 (LGPL-3.0-or-later), from
https://download.gnome.org/sources/vte/0.84/vte-0.84.0.tar.xz
(sha256 0414e31583836aeb7878da25f67c515f7e8879917ecc37c92e26b83e8d8fc3e3),
built by kindle/build-vte.sh with the patches in kindle/vte/ (both in
source.tar.gz).
